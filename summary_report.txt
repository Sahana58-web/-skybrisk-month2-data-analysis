Sales Data Analysis - Summary Report
Skybrisk Internship - Month 2

Dataset Overview:
The dataset contains 15 sales orders across 3 categories (Electronics,
Stationery, Furniture) and 4 regions (North, South, East, West), with
some missing values in the Quantity and Region columns.

Data Cleaning:
- Missing Quantity values were filled with the average quantity (6.15).
- Missing Region values were labeled as "Unknown" since they could not
  be inferred from the other columns.

Key Insights:
1. Electronics is by far the highest-earning category, driven mainly by
   high-priced items like laptops (Rs. 55,000 each).
2. North region generated the highest total sales (Rs. 113,550),
   followed by East and South.
3. There is a strong positive correlation (0.87) between Price and
   Total sales, meaning higher-priced items contribute more to revenue
   even when ordered in smaller quantities.
4. There is a mild negative correlation (-0.43) between Quantity and
   Price, suggesting that more expensive items (like laptops) tend to
   be ordered in smaller amounts, while cheaper items (like pens and
   notebooks) are ordered in bulk.
5. The average order value across all products is roughly Rs. 19,778.

Conclusion:
Electronics products, despite being ordered less frequently, are the
biggest driver of revenue. This kind of insight would help a business
focus marketing and stock priority on high-value categories.
